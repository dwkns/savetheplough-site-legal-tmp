Brockley Green, Hundon, Suffolk

“Do it by the book.”

What one of the new owners told residents about his plans for The Plough

We took that to mean following the planning process. Since then the owners have refitted the building, and they now market it for private hire and group stays. No planning application has been made.

We are asking the owners to open The Plough as a pub, or to apply for planning permission so that everyone gets a say.

Where things stand

  • No planning application has been made for The Plough. The council's planning register shows no application for the site since 1996.
  • The owners plan to open a coffee lounge and bar on 1 December 2026. It will be closed on Fridays and Saturdays "for private events".
  • Their brochure offers exclusive use of the whole site to groups of up to 24, with prices running to December 2028.
  • West Suffolk Council's planning enforcement team has a case for The Plough, reference EN/26/0160.

We last checked these facts on 28 October 2026. See the full timeline and sources.

Two ways to do it by the book

We are not asking for anything unusual. Either of these would do.

Open The Plough as a pub

A proper village pub, open to everyone, where you can walk in for a drink in the evening without booking. The Plough was that for around 170 years, and we would love to see it again.

or

Apply for planning permission

If the owners want The Plough to be something else, the proper route is a planning application. Neighbours see the plans and can comment. The council weighs up the road, the noise and the loss of the pub before it decides.

Why it matters to us

  • The bend

    The car park opens onto a double blind bend on Hundon Road. Events bring lots of cars arriving and leaving at once, and the owners' brochure says the site can take coaches.

  • Late nights

    The premises licence allows alcohol sales until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays. Those are the nights the owners have set aside for private events.

  • The walk to the pub

    There is no footway, no street lighting and no bus. The nearest other pubs are about 1.7 and 1.9 miles away by road. Without The Plough, Brockley Green has no pub you can walk to.

Listed as an Asset of Community Value

In July 2026 West Suffolk Council listed The Plough Inn as an Asset of Community Value. Residents asked Hundon Parish Council to nominate it, and the listing runs until 21 July 2031.
