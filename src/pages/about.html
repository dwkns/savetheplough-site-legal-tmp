About us

We are residents of Brockley Green and Hundon. Most of us would much rather be sitting in The Plough than running a website about it.

Who we are

Save the Hundon Plough is a group of local residents. Darrell Wilkins organises the campaign. About 20 residents backed the Asset of Community Value nomination in January 2026, and more have joined since.

We are not part of West Suffolk Council, Hundon Parish Council or any political party. Residents pay the small running costs of this website themselves.

To contact us, email Darrell at {{email}}.

How we check our facts

We aim to give a source and a date for every fact on this site. Where we can, we use the public record: the council's planning and licensing registers, its list of assets of community value, and what the owners have published themselves. Where something comes from the campaign organiser or other residents who saw it, we say so.

Our opinions are always in pink boxes marked "Our view", so you can tell them apart from the facts.

Corrections and right of reply

If you think anything on this site is wrong, email {{email}}. Tell us what you think is wrong and send any evidence you have. We will reply within 7 days. If we got something wrong, we will correct it and record the change below.

If the owners of The Plough would like to set out their position, we will publish a short statement from them on this site, next to our own. We will not change their words, except to remove anything unlawful.

Changes we have made

  • 28 October 2026, why the pub closed. An earlier version of our Asset of Community Value page gave a reason for the pub's closure in 2024. We cannot confirm that reason, so we have removed it.
  • 28 October 2026, consultation. An earlier version said residents were never consulted. The owners did talk to some residents about their plans. What has not happened is the formal consultation that comes with a planning application. We have changed the wording to say that.
