Other ways to help

You do not need to do all of these. One complaint in your own words is the most useful thing. The rest helps too.

  1. Make a complaint

    If you have not done it yet, this matters most. Say how what is happening at The Plough affects you. It takes about 10 minutes.

    Make a complaint

  2. Keep a record of events

    When there is an event, write down the date, the times and what you saw or heard. Take photos from a public road or your own property.

    Keep a record of an event

  3. Tell your councillors

    A short, polite email to your West Suffolk ward councillors, saying how the change affects you, helps them understand what residents think. You can find them on the council's website by searching for your postcode. You can also ask Hundon Parish Council to keep The Plough on its agenda.

  4. Get campaign updates

    We send occasional emails when something happens, such as news from the council or a planning application being made.

  5. Tell your neighbours

    Share savetheplough.com with people who live nearby or use the road. Each complaint should be that person's own, about how it affects them.

  6. If a planning application is made

    We will tell everyone on our updates list. You will then be able to comment on it on the council's planning register.

Please campaign fairly

  • Stick to what you have seen or heard yourself.
  • Be polite, in person and online.
  • Stay on public land or your own property.
  • Do not approach the owners, their staff, guests or suppliers about the campaign.
  • Do not post photos of people, or comments about anyone personally.

It keeps the campaign about the planning issue, which is where it is strongest.

If something is happening now

If anyone is in danger, call 999. For a non-emergency road problem, call the police on 101. For noise late at night, you can report it to West Suffolk Council's environmental health team.
