What has happened

The story of The Plough so far, in date order. Every fact has a source. Where we give our opinion, it is in a pink box marked "Our view".

Pink boxes are our opinion. Everything else is a fact we can show you. If you think we have something wrong, tell us and we will check it.

A village pub

  1. 1850s

    The first records of an inn

    The owners' own website says the building started life as the village school house, and that the first records of it as a coaching inn appear in the 1850s.

    Source: The Plough Country Lodge, About us page, seen 23 October 2026.

  2. From 1957

    A family-run pub

    The Rowlinson family ran The Plough from 1957. A larger restaurant and nine en suite guest rooms were added in the late 1980s. The pub was listed in the Good Pub Guide from 1986 and in the AA guide to pubs and inns from 1999.

    Sources: history supplied by David Rowlinson for the Asset of Community Value nomination, January 2026. The extensions are on the council's planning register, references E/87/4064/P and E/88/3682/P.

  3. 2022

    Platinum Jubilee

    The Plough held celebrations for the Queen's Platinum Jubilee, with live bands. Over the years it also hosted quiz nights, charity evenings, New Year's Eve dinners and meetings of the Round Table, Rotary and Lions.

    Source: the Asset of Community Value evidence, January 2026.

  4. October 2024

    The pub closes

    The Plough closed as a pub in October 2024.

    Source: Suffolk News, 28 September 2025.

New owners

  1. 2025

    "Do it by the book"

    Before the sale went through, one of the new owners told a resident that he would "do it by the book". He said the same thing later, at a meeting with residents, and said he wanted to take the community with him.

    Source: the campaign organiser and other residents who were there.

    Our view

    We took that to mean following the normal planning process if the use of the building changed. That is all we are asking for.

  2. 15 September 2025

    "No longer be managed as a public house or restaurant"

    The owners announced their plans on Facebook: "Whilst it will no longer be managed as a public house or restaurant, we are excited to announce our future plans of holding various milestone events, birthday celebrations and weddings."

    The same post said they planned to refurbish the rooms for short-term holiday lets and to hold seasonal public events, starting with a Winter Wonderland in December.

    Source: the owners' announcement on Facebook, 15 September 2025.

    Our view

    This describes a move away from a pub to a different kind of business. In our view, that is the kind of change that needs planning permission. West Suffolk Council decides.

  3. December 2025

    Winter Wonderland in the garden

    The owners ran a Winter Wonderland at The Plough with a synthetic ice rink, inflatables, festive stalls and mulled wine. Their poster gave the opening times as every Thursday to Sunday from 4 to 23 December, 3pm to 8pm.

    Source: the owners' posters on Facebook, November and December 2025.

2026

  1. 12 January 2026

    Residents ask for a listing

    About 20 residents asked Hundon Parish Council to nominate The Plough as an Asset of Community Value.

    Source: our letter to Hundon Parish Council, 12 January 2026.

  2. 27 June 2026

    A wedding reception

    A wedding reception was held at The Plough. The campaign organiser saw the car park fill, with cars parked along the entrance and on the verge, and a neighbour's access blocked.

    Source: the campaign organiser, who saw it and took these photos.

    Our view

    One event filled the site. We worry about what a regular programme of events would mean for a road with a double blind bend.

  3. 22 July 2026

    Listed as an Asset of Community Value

    West Suffolk Council listed The Plough Inn, Brockley Green, as an Asset of Community Value. The listing runs from 22 July 2026 to 21 July 2031.

    Source: West Suffolk Council, community right to challenge and bid.

  4. 6 August 2026

    Ready for hire

    The owners posted that the first phase of work was nearly complete, so the ground floor could be hired for "weddings, christenings, milestone celebrations". They said the accommodation, with 10 en suite bathrooms, was the second phase.

    Source: The Plough Country Lodge Facebook page, 6 August 2026.

  5. 17 August 2026

    New national policy protects pubs

    The government published a new National Planning Policy Framework. Its policy HC6 names public houses as key community facilities and says they should not be lost unless strict tests are met. Where a case rests on a pub no longer being viable, it asks for evidence of at least 12 months of marketing for its existing use.

    Source: National Planning Policy Framework, August 2026 (PDF), policy HC6.

  6. Summer and autumn 2026

    Residents contact the council

    Residents have sent complaints to West Suffolk Council's planning enforcement team since July 2026. The council has a planning enforcement case for The Plough, reference EN/26/0160.

    Source: correspondence with West Suffolk Council.

  7. October 2026

    A brochure to the end of 2028

    The owners' brochure, with prices from April 2027 to December 2028, offers "Exclusive use of the whole site and private grounds" for 10 to 24 guests, with self-catering accommodation. One night for up to 24 guests costs £2,200, and seven nights cost £6,000. In its booking terms, under the heading "Hen and stag parties", it says: "We are happy to accommodate all male or all female groups". It says the site can take "large vehicles including coaches". Extras include bouncy castles, an assault course, archery, karaoke hire and "It's a Knock Out" events.

    The brochure does not mention the coffee lounge and bar.

    Source: The Plough Country Lodge brochure and prices, April 2027 to December 2028, from the owners' website.

    Our view

    The brochure describes a holiday let and party venue for groups that book the whole site. In our view, that is now the main use of The Plough.

  8. 20 October 2026

    A coffee lounge and bar from 1 December

    The owners announced that a coffee lounge and bar will open on 1 December 2026. It will be open on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday from 10:30am to 5pm and closed on Mondays. The post says "Friday and Saturday Closed for private events" and "Kindly note that the restaurant will not be reopening."

    Source: The Plough Country Lodge Facebook page, October 2026, and the opening times page on their website.

    Our view

    We would be glad to see people back at The Plough. But a daytime café that closes on Fridays and Saturdays for private events is not, to us, the village pub.

    As we read the brochure's terms, the public cannot use the site while a group has booked all of it. So the café could only open on days with no such booking.

  9. 8 to 31 December 2026

    "Skate and Bounce" planned

    The owners are advertising a public "Skate and Bounce" event with a synthetic ice rink, inflatables and a "Licensed Bar Available for Adults", closed from 25 to 28 December.

    Source: The Plough Country Lodge Facebook page, October 2026.

  10. 28 October 2026

    What the public registers show

    West Suffolk Council's planning register shows no planning application for The Plough since 1996.

    The council's licensing register shows a current premises licence for The Plough Inn. It allows alcohol sales until midnight from Sunday to Thursday, and until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays.

    Sources: West Suffolk planning register and licence register, licence PL06372, both checked on 28 October 2026.
