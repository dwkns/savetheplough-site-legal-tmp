Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what we do with it.

Who we are

Save the Hundon Plough is a residents' campaign. Darrell Wilkins, the campaign organiser, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Contact him at {{email}}.

Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. If you withdraw consent after we have sent your complaint to the council, we cannot recall it, but you can ask the council to withdraw it.

Who we share it with

  • West Suffolk Council, when we enter your complaint for you, or when you have agreed that we can send your record of an event.
  • Companies that run this website for us: our web host, our email delivery service and our postcode lookup service. They act on our instructions and may store data outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires.

We never sell your information or share it with the owners of The Plough.

What the council does with your complaint

Once the council has your complaint, it is responsible for it. The council normally keeps the names of people who complain confidential. See West Suffolk Council's own privacy notice for details.

How long we keep it

  • Complaints and records of events: until 12 months after the council closes its enforcement case for The Plough, then we delete them.
  • Email updates: until you ask us to stop.
  • Emails to us: for as long as we need them to deal with what you asked.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or ask us to stop using it. Email {{email}}. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so loading a page does not send your details to anyone else.
