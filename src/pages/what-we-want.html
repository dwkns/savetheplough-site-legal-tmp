Keep the promise

One of the new owners told residents he would do it by the book and take the community with him. We are asking for exactly that, and nothing more.

What "by the book" means

When the main use of a building changes, for example from a pub to something quite different, planning permission is normally needed first. That is the book. It applies to everyone, and it exists so that neighbours get a say before things change, not after.

No planning application has been made for The Plough. So nobody has had that say yet.

Two ways to keep the promise

Open The Plough as a pub

By a pub we mean what most people round here would mean. A place that is open to everyone, in the evening as well as the day, where you can walk in for a drink without booking. Regulars, families and walkers all use it. The Plough was that for around 170 years.

Or apply for planning permission

If the owners want The Plough to be a venue for private hire and group stays, the proper route is a planning application. That would mean:

  • the plans are published, so everyone can see what is proposed
  • neighbours, the parish council and anyone else can comment
  • the council weighs up the road, the noise and the loss of the pub against national and local planning policy
  • if the council grants permission, it can set conditions, for example on hours, numbers or parking

What we are not asking for

We have nothing against weddings, parties or holiday lets. Plenty of places host them, with planning permission. We are not asking the council to punish anyone, and we are not asking for special treatment. We want the same process that everyone else follows.

If the owners apply

We will read the plans and comment on them, as anyone can. We expect plenty of neighbours will do the same. Then the council will decide. That is how it should work.

Until then

We will keep asking West Suffolk Council to look at what is happening at The Plough. That means complaints from residents in their own words, records of events with dates, and keeping our councillors informed.
