Questions people ask us

If your question is not here, email Darrell at darrell.wilkins@gmail.com.

Are you against the owners?

No. We have nothing against the owners or their right to run a business. One of them told residents he would "do it by the book" and take the community with him. We understood that to mean following the planning process, and that is what we are asking for.

What exactly are you asking for?

Either of two things. Open The Plough as a pub that anyone can walk into, or apply for planning permission for whatever the owners want it to be. A planning application lets neighbours see the plans and comment, and lets the council decide. See what we want.

Is what is happening at The Plough against the rules?

That is for West Suffolk Council to decide, not us. What we know is that no planning application has been made. We believe one is needed, because The Plough is no longer run as a pub and is marketed for private hire and group stays.

A coffee lounge and bar is opening. Is that not a pub again?

We will be glad to see people back at The Plough. But the owners say it will open from 10:30am to 5pm on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays, and will be closed on Fridays and Saturdays for private events. They also say the restaurant will not reopen.

Our view

A daytime café that closes for private events on Fridays and Saturdays is not the village pub we had. The council will decide what the main use of the site is.

Why complain rather than sign a petition?

A planning enforcement team looks at evidence about what is happening on a site. Complaints from people who are affected, in their own words and with dates, tell the team what is going on. A petition shows how people feel, but it is not evidence about the site.

Will the owners find out that I complained?

The council normally keeps the names of people who complain confidential. In rare cases it may have to share information, for example if a case goes to an appeal or to court. Our privacy notice explains what we do with your details.

Can I complain if I do not live next door?

Yes. Anyone affected can complain, for example if you use the road, live in the area or used the pub. Say how it affects you. A complaint about your own experience is more useful than one that repeats what other people have said.

I have already complained. Should I do it again?

Please do not send the same complaint twice. If something new happens, such as another event, write to the council's planning enforcement team with the date and what you saw, and quote case reference EN/26/0160. You can also keep a record of it here.

What is an Asset of Community Value?

It is a listing under the Localism Act 2011. If the owners decide to sell The Plough, a community group can say it wants to bid. The sale then has to wait while the group gets ready. It does not force the owners to reopen the pub or to sell. See our page about the listing.

What happens next?

The council's planning enforcement team decides what to do. It can ask the owners for information, invite them to apply for planning permission, or take formal action. It can also decide to take no action. We will post news on our updates page.

Who runs this website, and who pays for it?

Residents of Brockley Green and Hundon run it. Darrell Wilkins organises the campaign, and residents pay the small running costs themselves. We are not part of West Suffolk Council or any political party. More on our about page.

How do I know the information here is right?

We give a source for every fact, and we mark our opinions clearly. If you think something is wrong, tell us and we will check it and correct it. Read our corrections policy.
