Make a complaint to West Suffolk Council

Use this form to write a planning enforcement complaint about The Plough, in your own name.

We give you draft wording to start from. You then change it so that it says what you know and how you are affected. A complaint in your own words carries more weight than one that matches everyone else's.

It takes about 10 minutes.

What you need

  • your address, email address and phone number
  • any dates, notes or photos of things you have seen or heard yourself (if you have them)

What happens next

We enter your complaint on the council's online planning enforcement form, using your details. Each complaint says it was prepared with help from this website. The council will then contact you directly.

The council normally keeps the names of people who complain confidential. Read our privacy notice to see how we look after your details.
