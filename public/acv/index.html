The Plough is an Asset of Community Value

West Suffolk Council listed The Plough Inn as an Asset of Community Value on 22 July 2026. The listing lasts until 21 July 2031.

What the listing is

An Asset of Community Value is a building or piece of land that a council agrees is important to local people. The rules are in the Localism Act 2011.

To list a building, the council has to be satisfied of two things. First, that its main use, recently, furthered the social wellbeing or social interests of the local community. Second, that it could realistically do so again.

What the listing does

If the owners decide to sell The Plough, they must tell the council first. A local community group can then say it wants to bid, and the sale has to wait while the group gets ready.

What the listing does not do

It does not force the owners to reopen the pub, and it does not force them to sell. It does not decide whether planning permission is needed for what is happening at The Plough. That is a separate question for the council's planning team.

How it happened

In January 2026, about 20 residents asked Hundon Parish Council to nominate The Plough. Darrell Wilkins prepared the case on their behalf. He used the pub's history, supplied by former owner David Rowlinson, and guidance from CAMRA, the Campaign for Real Ale.

The council received the nomination on 10 June 2026 and listed The Plough Inn on 22 July 2026.

The case we made

  • An inn from the Victorian era, and family-run from 1957.
  • A larger restaurant and nine guest rooms from the late 1980s.
  • Quiz nights, charity evenings, New Year's Eve dinners, the 2022 Jubilee and meetings of the Round Table, Rotary and Lions.
  • A place for families, walkers, older residents and people who might otherwise be on their own.
  • Long listings in the Good Pub Guide, the AA guide to pubs and inns, and the Which? Guide to Country Pubs.
  • A building, layout and garden that are still well suited to a pub.
