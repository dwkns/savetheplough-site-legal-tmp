Updates

News from the campaign, newest first. For the full story, see what has happened.

  1. 28 October 2026

    A new look for this website

    We have rebuilt the site. There are new pages on what has happened, why it matters and how the planning rules work, with a source for every fact. The complaint form now asks for your own experience first, and you can keep a record of events.

  2. 21 October 2026

    Four more complaints sent to the council

    Four more residents sent complaints to West Suffolk Council's planning enforcement team this week. Thank you to everyone who has taken the time.

  3. 20 October 2026

    Coffee lounge and bar to open on 1 December

    The owners have announced a coffee lounge and bar, open from 10:30am to 5pm on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays. It will be closed on Mondays, and on Fridays and Saturdays for private events. They say the restaurant will not reopen. Read what we think.

  4. 17 August 2026

    New national policy protects pubs

    The government's new National Planning Policy Framework names pubs as key community facilities. It says they should not be lost unless strict tests are met. How the rules work.

  5. 22 July 2026

    The Plough is listed as an Asset of Community Value

    West Suffolk Council has listed The Plough Inn as an Asset of Community Value until 21 July 2031. Thank you to Hundon Parish Council and to the residents who backed the nomination. What the listing means.

  6. 27 June 2026

    Wedding reception at The Plough

    A wedding reception was held at The Plough. The car park filled, cars were parked on the verge and a neighbour's access was blocked. See the photos.
