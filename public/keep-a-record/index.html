Tips for a good record

  • Write it down on the day, while you remember.
  • Give the date and the times.
  • Say what you saw or heard yourself, not what someone told you.
  • Stay on public land or your own property.
  • Do not approach guests, staff or the owners.

If something needs dealing with now

If someone is in danger, call 999. For noise that is keeping you awake, you can report it to West Suffolk Council's environmental health team. The council may ask you to fill in its noise diary sheets.
